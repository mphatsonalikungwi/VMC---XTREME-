Gym rules.

Good training culture keeps VMC Xtreme safe, clean and welcoming for everyone.

01Wear appropriate workout clothes.
02Wipe down equipment after use.
03Return dumbbells, plates, and gear to proper places.
04Share equipment, avoid hogging machines during rest.
05Follow time limits on cardio during busy hours.
06Use equipment safely as instructed.
07Dropping weights is strictly prohibited.
08Keep phone use brief, no speakerphones.
09Bring a towel and maintain personal hygiene.
10Stay hydrated with a water bottle.
11Respect other members' space and privacy.
12Ask before giving unsolicited advice.
13Report damaged equipment immediately.
14Follow policies on food, photography, guest access.
15Be courteous to staff and members.
