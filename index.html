Member standards

Train hard. Respect the room.

The gym works better when everyone takes responsibility for the space and for one another.

01

Wear appropriate workout clothes and closed-toe athletic shoes.

02

Wipe equipment after use and return weights and equipment to their places.

03

Share equipment and follow cardio time limits during busy periods.

04

Use equipment as instructed and never drop weights.

05

Keep phone use brief and avoid loud conversations or speakerphone use.

06

Bring a towel, maintain good hygiene and stay hydrated.

07

Respect other members' personal space, privacy and training time.

08

Report damaged equipment or unsafe conditions to staff.

09

Follow VMC policies on food, photography and guest access.

10

Be courteous to staff and other members.

Questions

Before your first session.

A few useful answers for new and returning members.

Do I need experience to join?

No. Beginners are welcome. Start with a comfortable pace and ask staff when you need guidance.

Can I train twice in one day?

Yes. The double membership option covers both the morning and evening sessions on the same day.

How do I register?

Use the Join VMC button, complete the membership form and follow the payment instructions. Your membership remains pending until VMC verifies payment.

How can I contact the gym?

Call +265 991 203 382 or use WhatsApp. You can also visit VMC Xtreme in Chilinde 1, Sankhawekha.
