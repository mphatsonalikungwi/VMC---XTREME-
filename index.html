Member standards

VMC Xtreme Gym Rules

Every member is expected to help keep the gym safe, respectful, clean and enjoyable for everyone.

01

Wear appropriate workout clothes.

02

Wipe down equipment after use with the provided disinfectant.

03

Return dumbbells, plates, and other equipment to their proper places.

04

Share equipment and avoid occupying a machine during long rest periods.

05

Follow time limits on cardio machines during busy hours.

06

Use equipment as instructed to reduce the risk of injury.

07

Dropping weights is strictly prohibited.

08

Keep phone use brief and avoid loud conversations or speakerphone use.

09

Bring a towel and maintain good personal hygiene.

10

Stay hydrated by bringing a water bottle.

11

Respect other members' personal space and privacy.

12

Ask before giving advice or interrupting someone else's workout.

13

Report damaged equipment or unsafe conditions to staff.

14

Follow the gym's policies on food, photography, and guest access.

15

Be courteous to staff and other gym members.

Member responsibility: By registering, members will be required to acknowledge that they have read and agree to follow these gym rules.
Questions

Frequently asked.

Is VMC suitable for beginners?

Yes — our personal training is built around your experience level, so first-time gym-goers get just as much guidance as members who've trained for years.

What is the difference between single and double sessions?

A single session covers one visit in a day, either morning or evening. A double session covers both sessions on the same day.

Is drinking water available?

Yes — a 500ml bottle is available on site for K1,000.

What should I bring?

Appropriate workout clothing, closed-toe athletic shoes, and a water bottle or towel.
